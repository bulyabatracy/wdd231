Welcome to Explore Kampala

Kampala is Uganda's vibrant capital city, known for its friendly people, rich cultural heritage, exciting nightlife, beautiful hills, and delicious local cuisine. Whether you are visiting for business, adventure, or relaxation, our website helps you discover the very best places to explore.

Featured Attractions

Uganda National Mosque

Enjoy one of the best panoramic views of Kampala from the mosque tower.

Kasubi Tombs

Discover Uganda's UNESCO World Heritage Site and learn about Buganda Kingdom history.

Ndere Cultural Centre

Experience traditional Ugandan music, dance and cultural performances.
