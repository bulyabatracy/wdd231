Site Plan: Visit Kampala Hub

Site Name

Visit Kampala Hub

This name was selected because it is clear, welcoming, and location-specific. "Visit" invites tourists, "Kampala" targets the capital city, and "Hub" shows it is a central place for all travel information. It is good for branding and SEO for people searching things to do in Kampala.

Optional Domain: visitkampalahub.ug

Site Purpose

The purpose of Visit Kampala Hub is to provide international and local tourists with a complete guide to experiencing Kampala and nearby attractions in Uganda. The site will provide information on top attractions, cultural experiences, restaurant recommendations, accommodation options, and safety tips. It will also include an events calendar and a booking inquiry form to connect visitors with local tour guides and services.

Scenarios - Target Audience Questions

These are questions that represent what visitors to the site will be asking:

  • What are the top 5 must-see attractions in Kampala for a first-time visitor with only 2 days?
  • Where can I find safe and affordable restaurants that serve authentic Ugandan food near the city center?

Color Scheme

The color palette is inspired by Uganda: nature, sunshine, and the national flag. It creates a warm and trustworthy feeling for travelers.

  • Primary - #2E7D32 Forest Green: Used for headings, navigation background, and footer background. Represents Uganda's national parks and nature.
  • Secondary - #FFA000 Amber: Used for links, buttons, and accent icons. Represents sunshine and safari experiences.
  • Accent - #D32F2F Red: Used for call-to-action buttons and important highlights. Represents the red in the Ugandan flag.
  • Background - #FAFAFA Off-white: Used for the main page background to keep content readable.

Typography

Two fonts will be used to create an elegant yet friendly tourism brand.

  • Heading Font: Playfair Display - Used for all h1, h2, and h3 headings. It is elegant and evokes a sense of travel and adventure for site titles.
  • Body Font: Lato - Used for all paragraph text, lists, and navigation links. It is highly legible for reading travel guides and details on mobile.
